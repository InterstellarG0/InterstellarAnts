# Interstellar Ants

Walk a living 3D ant colony in first person. The ants remember you.

Interstellar Ants is a walkable 3D ant colony simulation. You spawn in the Neon Nest and explore a seeded, endless world on foot: worker crews build, named NPCs remember your conversations, a night watch patrols by lamplight, scout parties roam the wilds, and a telescope ant watches a signal from Andromeda that gets clearer every night.

Free to play, pay what you want: https://interstellarg0.itch.io/interstellar-ants

Play it

This repo holds the game as a single self-contained HTML file. No install, no server, no build step.

1. Download `interstellar-ants-walkable-3d.html`
2. Open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox, Safari)
3. Click **Enter Colony** and walk

The file works offline. The only thing it reaches for online is the page font; everything else, including the Three.js engine and the star catalog, is baked in.

Gamepad note:** open the file directly as a top-level tab for controller support. Xbox controllers work over USB; some browsers block gamepad access inside embedded frames.

What's in the colony

- A living colony sim. Population grows over time, faster when structures rise. Daily events, spore storms, crystal fever, sinkholes, hatchings, wandering broods, all logged in the Colony Log.
- NPCs with memory. Founder Anthony, the Scout, and the Lens Keeper remember talks, trust, pledges, lanterns lit, sites visited, and beams raised.
- Day and night. Day crews work 07:00 to 19:00 and sleep in the nest ring at night. The night watch takes over: Night Captain VESPER and two crews of five patrol by lamplight until dawn.
- Ranks. Night shift: Night Tender, Night Warden, Shift Lead, Night Captain. Scouts: Scout, Pathfinder, Scout Lead, Ranger. Ants earn their stripes in the Colony Log.
- Scout parties. Dawnline and Farline run wide seeded circuits 50 to 95 units out with nest breaks between loops. Ranger TALON rides the widest circuit through every landmark.
- Assign the crew (v2.0). Walk up to any ant and hand them an open scaffold that needs finishing. Orders hold overnight, release when the scaffold tops out, and recovering ants can't be ordered.
- Sky Harbor. A landing pad site with beacon posts, a sky beam, and a harbor console. Supply pods parachute in, survey drones map the wilds, meteor showers streak overhead, and deep-sky freighters cross with running lights.
- The Real Sky. The 105 brightest stars in Earth's sky at their true positions, with real magnitudes and spectral colors from the open Yale Bright Star Catalog, plus constellation lines, halos on the brightest twelve, and a Milky Way band on the true galactic plane. At the Lens Keeper's Observatory the chart opens as a hologram.
- The Creative Lab. A warm room where tired ants recover and create. After storms, worn ants drift in to rest, and rested ants sometimes leave a named dream piece on the quiet shelf. Kept, counted, saved, never interpreted.
- Save and resume. Your whole colony saves to a downloadable file (SAVE chip) and restores from it (LOAD chip). Old save files keep loading across versions.
